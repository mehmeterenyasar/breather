## Detailed description

```
Breather is a calm focus timer and break companion for people who work at a desk. One click to start, and as much depth as you want once you open the settings.

◆ FOUR WAYS TO WORK
• Pomodoro — every duration and the long-break rhythm are yours to set
• Stopwatch — no time limit; the session is saved when you finish
• Countdown — quick 5/10/15/30/45/60 minute presets plus a custom length
• Flowtime — starts like a stopwatch and, when you stop, suggests a break proportional to the time you worked

◆ HEALTH BREAKS
• Eye break — every 20 minutes, look 6 metres away for 20 seconds
• Breathing break — box breathing, 4-7-8, coherent breathing, physiological sigh and a custom pattern, with an animated guide
• Posture break — short stretches for neck, shoulders, wrists and back
• Water break — a simple counter against a daily goal

Each break can be turned on and off separately, with its own interval and length. They never interrupt a Pomodoro focus session: pending breaks are offered during the Pomodoro break instead.

◆ REMINDERS THAT RESPECT YOU
• Quick mute: 30 minutes, 1 hour, 2 hours or until the end of the day
• Working hours: set per weekday; nothing is sent outside them
• Idle detection: when you step away the timer pauses and break counters reset; on your return it asks whether to add that time to the session
• Meeting detection (optional): reminders stay quiet while a Meet, Zoom or Teams tab is in front

◆ HISTORY AND STATISTICS
• Daily focus time as a stacked bar chart by tag, with your goal line
• A year-at-a-glance heatmap
• A weekday × hour heatmap showing your most productive hours
• Tag distribution, break compliance and a session list
• Forgot to start the timer? Add the session by hand

◆ YOUR DATA IS YOURS
• No account, no server, no analytics, no ads
• The extension makes no network requests at all — even the typeface is bundled
• Export to JSON or CSV whenever you like, or delete everything in one place

◆ DETAILS
• Turkish and English
• Light and dark themes, six accent colours
• No audio files: notification tones are generated in the browser
• Keyboard shortcuts (changeable from your browser's shortcuts page)
• Respects reduced-motion preferences and works with screen readers
• Injects nothing into the pages you visit

Privacy policy: see PRIVACY.md in the extension's repository
```

## Privacy section

- **Single purpose:** Letting the user time focus sessions and take regular health breaks.
- **Data collection:** No data is collected and none is transmitted.
- **Remote code:** Not used.
